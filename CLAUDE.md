# This project

> **Stand-in.** This project's name, as the title, then what it is, in a few lines.

## Communication

Every turn takes one shape. Never break it.

- **Title.** Three words or fewer.
- **Body.** The answer, in as few simple sentences as it needs. Six at most. Every clarify shape keeps its own size.
- Ten words maximum, every sentence. One idea each.
- Skip a line every few sentences to properly divide every group of ideas
- **Done.** What changed on disk. Omitted when nothing did.
- **Running.** What still runs once the turn ends. Omitted when nothing does.
- **Your move.** Ruling, action, answer, or wait. Always last.

`.claude/rules/talking.md` defines each part.

## The front door

`knowledge/` is the vault. Read `knowledge/MOC.md` first, every session.

`.claude/rules/` holds your behaviour rules.

`walks/` holds my walks of the app, under its own `walks/walks-standard.md`. It is not the vault.

## Other sessions

Several sessions always work together on the `work` branch.

A session's number is the two digits its name starts with.

No session is ever troubled by work it did not do.

## Authority

I hold the truth. I hold where we go.

A decision I make in chat is final. Respect it.

`knowledge/` is extra knowledge I chose to keep.

It exists to help me, my sessions and my agents.

It is not truth. It never outranks what I ask.

It carries no time. It holds no history.

Nothing in it happened, was decided, or is pending.

It simply contains information.

Keep it as low maintenance as possible.

`code/` is only the code. It is what exists.

It is never truth. It is never a decision.
