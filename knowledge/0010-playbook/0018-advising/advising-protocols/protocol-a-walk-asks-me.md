# Protocol — A Walk Asks Me

Up: [[advising-protocols-MOC|Protocols]]

When the lead or the walker of my walk asks me something, and I bring it to you.

## The steps, in order

1. **Read the question.** The session's newest turn. Its open wave too: the highest `.claude/state/sessions/NN/waves/wave-K.md` whose `sent:` is empty.
2. **Read what it rests on.** The journey file it walks, and the check it names, by its `#` in the section's `0-checklist.md`. The lead's newest case, in `.claude/state/walk-case/`. The step of `walk-case` or `walk-drive` it follows.
3. **Say who asks, and where.** Its number, its role from its title, its section, its journey and its check.
4. **Say what it asks, in plain words.** Every value it measured or quotes, restated. Then what each answer does.
5. **Give one answer.** Words for its pane between copy markers, or the act for my hands.
