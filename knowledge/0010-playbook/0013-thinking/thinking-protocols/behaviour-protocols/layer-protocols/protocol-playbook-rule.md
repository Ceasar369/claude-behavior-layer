# Protocol — Playbook Rule

Up: [[layer-protocols-MOC|Layer Protocols]]

When the subject is a rule for how sessions behave.

## The parts it touches

Behaviour. `CLAUDE.md`, `.claude/rules/`, `knowledge/0010-playbook/`, and any skill carrying the rule.

[[the-behaviour-axioms|The Behaviour Axioms]] hold the floor.

## The questions, in order

1. Is it written already? Search its words, then its meaning.
2. Who needs it? Every session, one kind of subject, or one skill.
3. Is it a principle, free of any domain?
4. Is it a fact, with no time and no exception?
5. What reasons from the old meaning? `.claude/rules/thinking.md` rules the mapping.

## The lessons

- `.claude/rules/` holds principles only.
- A new rule joins `talking.md` or `thinking.md`. Only a new subject opens a third file.
- Every rule is stated once. A rule `CLAUDE.md` states is not restated.
- A one-off stays a one-off. It never rewrites a rule, and is never written.
- A rule I never asked for is never added.
