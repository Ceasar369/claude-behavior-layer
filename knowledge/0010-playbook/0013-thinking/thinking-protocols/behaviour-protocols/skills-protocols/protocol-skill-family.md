# Protocol — Skill Family

Up: [[skills-protocols-MOC|Skills Protocols]]

When the subject is a family of skills: a router and its children.

## The parts it touches

Behaviour. The router, each child, and any page 1100 draws for the family.

[[the-behaviour-axioms|The Behaviour Axioms]] hold the floor.

## The questions, in order

1. What is the end use case? Walk one run, from my first word to the result.
2. What is the one door? The router I type. Only it combines the children.
3. What does each child do alone? Any session may call it.
4. Who decides, at each step? A child never decides what is mine.
5. Which member takes a digit? Only one I type all day.
6. Does a fresh session walk me through it? Test it in a new pane.

## The lessons

- One door beats many.
- A child never calls a sibling. Only the router combines them.
- No two members collide on a digit. A child never inherits its router's.
- A family works for me alone, or for another session.
