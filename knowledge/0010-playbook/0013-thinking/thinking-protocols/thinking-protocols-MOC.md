Every thinking protocol, by the kind of subject it serves.

A thinking protocol is a written way to think one kind of subject.

## Children

- [[protocol-new-protocol|New Protocol]] — When the subject is a new thinking protocol to create.
- [[behaviour-protocols-MOC|Behaviour Protocols]] — When the subject is behaviour: a skill, a page, a session, or the layer itself.

## Admission rule

A protocol enters here when I agree to it.

The new protocol sits here, at the root. Every other sits in its kind's folder.

Every line opens with the kind of subject it serves.
