# Protocol — Glance

Up: [[prototyping-protocols-MOC|Protocols]]

When I name Glance, or no level at all. The default: a quick look, for minutes and few tokens.

## The generation

One variation.

Several looks asked share its one variation, side by side. A look is one take on a thing. Looks compete in groups, and one wins each group. A variation may hold several groups, each named by a letter.

A variation may show one design in sections: a part, a viewer or a state, each on its own. Sections never compete. Each is judged. A variation may hold groups of looks and sections together: a look with no group is only shown, as a section is.

Each look or section wears its number. A picture inside a section wears its section's number, then its own: 3.1. Chat names what each number shows.

The session writes it itself. No agent.

Its errors are checked at one width: the first I asked for, or the browser's own. An error → the session fixes its own file, then checks it again.

## The lessons

- Most looks need one quick variation. A higher level is mine to ask for.
- Before the first generation, nothing is asked: my words are the round.
