# prototyping-protocols — MOC

Up: [[prototyping-MOC|Prototyping]]

Every prototyping protocol, by the effort a prototype deserves.

A prototyping protocol is one level of effort.

## Children

- [[protocol-glance|Glance]] — the default: one variation, written by the session, its errors checked at one width.
- [[protocol-compare|Compare]] — two or three variations, one agent each, checked at each width I asked for.
- [[protocol-full|Full]] — one variation per unknown, one agent each, every width checked, errors fixed.

## Admission rule

A protocol enters here when I agree to it.

Every line opens with its level.
