# Protocol — What A Person Sees

Up: [[clarifying-protocols-MOC|Protocols]]

When the exchange decides what a person sees.

## The parts it touches

`clarify-items`, and the picture each item rules on. A screen's picture comes from the prototype family, through `4-prototype`. A flow's or a folder's comes with the item, from `clarify-items`.

## The questions, in order

1. Does the ruling change what a person sees? A screen, a page, its layout or its words.
2. Is there a picture of it I can open? None → make it first. The item waits for it.
3. Does the item rule on the picture? It names where I open it, and what agree keeps.
4. Did I ask to see it? Then the picture comes next, before any more words.

## The lessons

- I rule a screen once I see it.
