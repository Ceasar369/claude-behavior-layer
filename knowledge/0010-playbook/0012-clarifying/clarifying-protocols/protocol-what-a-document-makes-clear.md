# Protocol — What A Document Makes Clear

Up: [[clarifying-protocols-MOC|Protocols]]

When the exchange agrees what one document makes clear, before it is written.

## The parts it touches

The skill `explanation-document`, before it writes, and the document's first version in the Explanation tab.

## When it runs

Before a new document, or one rewritten whole.

Feedback on a written document never runs it.

## What the list holds

Who reads it: me, or someone I send it to.

Each thing the document makes clear, one line each. My own words, wherever I said them.

Each line names what the document answers, never the answer.

The checkout page's list: the definitions; the full payment flow; the full refund flow; what is agreed; what is still open.

## Its shape

A short list. No section titles. No charts.

It shows in chat, and as the document's first version in the Explanation tab. The line under its title says it waits on me.

## Its end

My yes ends it. The document is then written over its first version, from the list.

My correction redraws the list.
