# The Stages

Up: [[assisting-MOC|Assisting]]

How work splits into stages, and which sessions run together.

## One test

A stage holds sessions that run in parallel.

They never harm, interrupt or slow one another. That is the only test.

Whatever can run in parallel runs in parallel. I say when I want otherwise.

A stage holds nine sessions at most.

The nine counts the plan's missions and their helpers. Any other session I have one of them start is my own call.

[[protocol-sizing-a-stage|Sizing A Stage]] applies the test, mission by mission.

## Two kinds of plan

A plan runs in order, or it is open. I name its kind when the team starts. A plan I never named open runs in order.

In order, a stage goes out once what it needs has stopped. It closes once its sessions stop.

Open, a stage is a name grouping its missions. A mission goes out once I rule it, into any stage, running or earlier, as soon as a seat is free.

A mission I place nowhere joins the stage filling: the last stage, in the plan's order, holding a mission that went out, A before any. Once it reaches the cap, the next mission starts the stage after.

My "move on" starts the stage after at once. It is never questioned.

An open stage closes only when I close it. The plan ends only when I end it.

## The plan records stages, never waits

The reasoning is the assistant's own.

The plan names each stage and its missions. Nothing links two missions.
