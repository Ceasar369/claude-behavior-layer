# autonomous-driving-protocols — MOC

Up: [[assisting-protocols-MOC|Protocols]]

Every protocol for an autonomous drive: a drive prepared whole before it starts, so nothing waits on my press or my ruling.

An autonomous drive runs over one session, one team, or several teams. One session is a plan of one mission. Several teams go through my advisor.

The regular drive is its second position, only on my word: each ruling of mine reaches me at once. It is [[driving-protocols-MOC|Driving Protocols]]. An autonomous drive runs each plan by those protocols. These add only what differs.
