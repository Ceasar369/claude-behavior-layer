# Protocol — Preparing An Autonomous Drive

Up: [[autonomous-driving-protocols-MOC|Autonomous Driving Protocols]]

Before an autonomous drive starts, with me, until its check holds.

## The steps, in order

1. **Ask whether the work spans several teams.** One session or one team → it runs here. Several teams an advisor already holds → the discussion moves to that advisor, and nothing launches. Several teams and no advisor → you become their advisor, keeping this whole conversation. Skill: `autonomous-drive`, then `my-advisor`.
2. **Write every plan first.** One session is a plan of one mission. One team is your own plan. Several teams are your plan of teams: [[protocol-planning-teams|Planning Teams]]. Each team not launched yet has its plan written in your folder, for its assistant to take at launch. A plan runs in order: an open plan closes on my word alone. Skills: `assistant-stages` and `autonomous-drive`.
3. **Write every mission whole, with its objectives.** None starts as notes. Skill: `assistant-mission`.
4. **Make every objective a `Run:` line.** Each mission's objectives, each finish line, and each launch-list line. A `Yours:` line is proven before the drive, or its mission stays in a regular drive. Skills: `assistant-mission`, `autonomous-drive`.
5. **Keep out every mission that needs me.** No thinking, prototype, discovery or walk with me. A team holding one runs a regular drive, and its launch turn says why. Skill: `autonomous-drive`.
6. **Set every launch's account, model and effort.** Each mission, and each team's row. Each goes on the account with the most left, on a model its account is not pinned against. Skill: `autonomous-drive`.
7. **Keep each pause point I name.** After a stage, after a mission, or before a team launches. The check lists each. Skill: `autonomous-drive`.
8. **Send every wave.** Yours, and each launched team's. Every ruling the drive can see coming reaches me here, before the start. Skill: `clarify-items`.
9. **Run the check.** A failing line names its fix. Fix each, then run it again. Every line holds → start the drive. Skill: `autonomous-drive`.

## What reaches me

The check, whole, with each account's usage: only the windows that apply, each its percent used and its reset. The drive starts only once every line holds.
