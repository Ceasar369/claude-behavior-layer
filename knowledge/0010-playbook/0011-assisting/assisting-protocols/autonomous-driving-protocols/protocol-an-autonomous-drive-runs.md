# Protocol — An Autonomous Drive Runs

Up: [[autonomous-driving-protocols-MOC|Autonomous Driving Protocols]]

From an autonomous drive's start until its last plan ends.

## The steps, in order

1. **Drive each plan whole.** Every moment follows [[driving-protocols-MOC|Driving Protocols]]. Each stage's launch page fires itself, each row on its mission's account, model and effort. Skill: `assistant-drive`.
2. **Launch each team waiting on nothing, at the start.** Its row alone on your launch page, then fired. Nobody presses. Skill: `autonomous-drive`.
3. **Follow no team. Its lead types into your pane at each stage's close and its plan's end.** Each time, run each red line's command. It prints what its line says → the line turns green. Skills: `message`, then `autonomous-drive`.
4. **Launch a waiting team once its launch list is green.** As in step 2. Skill: `autonomous-drive`.
5. **A team launched inside the drive takes its plan, and drives at once.** No plan wave: its plan is already agreed. Skill: `autonomous-drive`.
6. **Extra work launches on System's defaults.** Its mission comes from rulings you already hold, `Run:` objectives only. Its account is System's default, unless capped: then the account with the most left. A ruling it needs is taken as step 7 says. Skill: `autonomous-drive`.
7. **A ruling of mine is taken by the driver.** A lead that needs one types it into the driver's pane. The driver takes its own recommendation, carries it, and records it in one wave per team, for my review at that team's end. Only these wait for me: anything shipped, committed, pushed or deployed, anything deleted, any money moved, and any step only my hands can take. Every piece of work they do not block goes on. Skills: `autonomous-drive`, `message`.
8. **A capped session waits for its reset.** Skill: `session-control`.
9. **A pause point pauses it.** [[protocol-pause-and-resume|Pause And Resume]]. Before a team, its row launches nothing until "Resume the drive." Skill: `autonomous-drive`.

## What reaches me

Nothing while it runs, but each pause point reached, with what finished and where to look, and what step 7 keeps for me. Each ruling taken for me waits in its team's wave, for that team's end.
