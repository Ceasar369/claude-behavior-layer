# driving-protocols — MOC

Up: [[assisting-protocols-MOC|Protocols]]

Every protocol for driven work: when I hand you sessions, until the last one stops.

## Children

- [[protocol-taking-the-drive|Taking The Drive]] — driven work, when I hand you a session, a stage or my whole plan.
- [[protocol-hearing-me-mid-drive|Hearing Me Mid-Drive]] — driven work, while you wait between finishes.
- [[protocol-a-session-finishes|A Session Finishes]] — driven work, when a session you drive answers, asks or stops working.
- [[protocol-closing-a-stage|Closing A Stage]] — driven work, when every session of a stage has stopped, or I close it.
- [[protocol-kicking-off-the-next-stage|Kicking Off The Next Stage]] — driven work, while a stage runs, and when a mission starts, once what it needs has stopped, or I rule it in an open plan.
- [[protocol-handing-itself-over|Handing Itself Over]] — driven work, when your context passes System's threshold.
- [[protocol-handing-over-what-you-drive|Handing Over What You Drive]] — driven work, when a session you drive passes System's threshold.
- [[protocol-stuck|Stuck]] — driven work, when something needs what only I can give.
- [[protocol-pause-and-resume|Pause And Resume]] — driven work, when I pause your drive, or resume it.
- [[protocol-ending-the-plan|Ending The Plan]] — driven work, when the last session of a drive stops, or I end an open plan.

## Admission rule

A protocol enters here when I agree to it.

Every line opens with the kind of work it serves.
