# Protocol — Handing Itself Over

Up: [[driving-protocols-MOC|Driving Protocols]]

When your context passes System's threshold while you drive.

## The steps, in order

1. **Read your own context after each moment.** The same % the Handover page reads. Skill: `session-control`.
2. **Past System's threshold, hand over between moments.** Never in the middle of a moment. The threshold is the one System sets for the Handover page. Skill: `session-control`.
3. **Run `session-handover` yourself.** On the order's next account, and the model and effort your seat was named, else System's. No page, and no press. Skill: `session-handover`.
4. **Your successor's first message ends with the drive your seat last took.** `/1-assistant drive plan`, `/1-assistant drive B` or `/1-assistant drive C1`, in place of `/assistant-successor`. Skill: `handover-resume`.
5. **Your successor drives on.** It takes the seat, reads every handover copy, then runs that drive line, as if I had asked for the drive. [[protocol-taking-the-drive|Taking The Drive]] then rules what it does first, and it arms the wait again. Skill: `0-session-start`, then `1-assistant`.
6. **A failed handover leaves you in the seat.** Skill: `session-control`.

## What reaches me

Nothing. Only a failed handover, through [[protocol-stuck|Stuck]].
