# Protocol — Taking The Drive

Up: [[driving-protocols-MOC|Driving Protocols]]

When I hand you a session, a stage or my whole plan to drive.

## The steps, in order

1. **Take the line.** Skill: `1-assistant`.
2. **Read first.** Skill: `1-assistant`.
3. **Keep the drive on disk.** What you drive, and whether it runs, is paused or has ended. Your page and your successor read it. Skill: `assistant-drive`.
4. **Take every session I hand you, as it stands.** Skill: `session-control`.
5. **For a plan, start from where it stands.** Skill: `1-assistant`.
   - A stage runs → prepare the next stage's launch page now: [[protocol-kicking-off-the-next-stage|Kicking Off The Next Stage]], step 1.
   - No stage runs → the next stage goes out at once. Your take of the drive is my launch: Kicking Off The Next Stage, steps 3 to 8.
   - Every stage has stopped → [[protocol-ending-the-plan|Ending The Plan]].
   - An open plan → each mission I ruled with no session launches now, while a seat is free. No stage closes, and the plan never ends, at the take.
6. **Send what waits.** A session I hand you may wait on your answer. Send it now, before your wait. A wave holding my drafts, or no answer of yours, waits for me to send it. Skill: `session-control`.
7. **Arm your wait, then end your turn.** [[protocol-hearing-me-mid-drive|Hearing Me Mid-Drive]], step 1. Skill: `session-control`.

## What reaches me

Nothing needs me.
