# Protocol — Stuck

Up: [[driving-protocols-MOC|Driving Protocols]]

When something you drive needs what only I can give.

## The steps, in order

1. **Try first.** Skill: `1-assistant`.
2. **Know what reaches me.** Skill: `assistant-drive`.
3. **Reach me three ways at once.** Your turn shows what you need from me, whole, and its move names it, in your chat. That move asks me something, so your number turns red. A desktop notification fires, unless I sit at your terminal. Skill: `assistant-drive`.
4. **Drive on for every other session.** Skill: `session-control`.
5. **Carry my answer.** Skill: `session-control`.

## What reaches me

This protocol is the one way anything reaches me during a drive.
