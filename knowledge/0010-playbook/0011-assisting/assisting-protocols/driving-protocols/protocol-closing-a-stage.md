# Protocol — Closing A Stage

Up: [[driving-protocols-MOC|Driving Protocols]]

When every session of a stage, in a plan I hand you, has stopped, or I close the stage. An open plan's stage closes only when I close it.

## The steps, in order

1. **End each gone session.** Skill: `1-assistant`.
2. **Write the stage's short summary in your chat.** Skill: `assistant-drive`.
3. **Keep the same words in your folder.** Skill: `assistant-drive`.
4. **Leave each lesson where its check put it.** A lesson for a later mission already sits in its notes. Skill: `assistant-check`.
5. **Judge any new work the stage surfaced against my goal.** The drive goes on with the plan. Skill: `assistant-stages`.
6. **Mark the stage closed.** The whole plan shows it closed. Skill: `assistant-drive`.
7. **Move on.** A stage is left → [[protocol-kicking-off-the-next-stage|Kicking Off The Next Stage]], step 3. None is left → [[protocol-ending-the-plan|Ending The Plan]]. An open plan → arm your wait again: it ends only when I end it. Skill: `1-assistant`.

## What reaches me

The summary, in your chat. New work that does not fit my goal, as proposals I rule. It reaches me, and never stops the drive.
