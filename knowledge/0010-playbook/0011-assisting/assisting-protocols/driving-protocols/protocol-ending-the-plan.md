# Protocol — Ending The Plan

Up: [[driving-protocols-MOC|Driving Protocols]]

When the last session of a drive I hand you stops, or I end an open plan.

## The steps, in order

1. **Close the last stage as any other:** [[protocol-closing-a-stage|Closing A Stage]]. Skill: `assistant-drive`.
2. **Check that the end holds.** Every stage done. In an open plan, every stage that went out closed, on my end. Every session of the plan stopped, and its panes closed. Each mission's work kept. Every stage's summary kept. A session still waiting on me holds the end until I rule. Skills: `1-assistant` and `assistant-drive`.
3. **Mark the drive ended.** The whole plan shows it ended. Skill: `assistant-drive`.
4. **Say it in one line.** Skill: `assistant-drive`.
5. **Stay ready to talk with me.** Skill: `1-assistant`.

## What reaches me

One line: the plan is done. Any proposal still waiting stays on your page, mine to rule.
