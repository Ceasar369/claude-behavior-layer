# night-driving-protocols — MOC

Up: [[assisting-protocols-MOC|Protocols]]

Every protocol for a night drive: a drive prepared whole before it starts, so nothing waits on my press.

A night drive runs over one session, one team, or several teams. One session is a plan of one mission. Several teams go through my advisor.

The regular drive is [[driving-protocols-MOC|Driving Protocols]]. A night drive runs each plan by those protocols. These add only what differs.

## Children

- [[protocol-preparing-a-night-drive|Preparing A Night Drive]] — night-driven work, before it starts, with me, until its check holds.
- [[protocol-a-night-drive-runs|A Night Drive Runs]] — night-driven work, from its start until its last plan ends.
- [[protocol-ending-a-night-drive|Ending A Night Drive]] — night-driven work, when its last plan ends.

## Admission rule

A protocol enters here when I agree to it.

Every line opens with the kind of work it serves.
