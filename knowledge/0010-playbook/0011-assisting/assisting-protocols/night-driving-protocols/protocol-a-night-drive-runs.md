# Protocol — A Night Drive Runs

Up: [[night-driving-protocols-MOC|Night Driving Protocols]]

From a night drive's start until its last plan ends.

## The steps, in order

1. **Drive each plan whole.** Every moment follows [[driving-protocols-MOC|Driving Protocols]]. Each stage's launch page fires itself, each row on its mission's account, model and effort. Skill: `assistant-drive`.
2. **Launch each team waiting on nothing, at the start.** Its row alone on your launch page, then fired. Nobody presses. Skill: `night-drive`.
3. **Follow each team a launch list waits on.** At each of its turns, run each red line's command. It prints what its line says → the line turns green. Skills: `follow`, then `night-drive`.
4. **Launch a waiting team once its launch list is green.** As in step 2. Skill: `night-drive`.
5. **A team launched inside the drive takes its plan, and drives at once.** No plan wave: its plan is already agreed. Skill: `night-drive`.
6. **Extra work launches on System's defaults.** Its mission comes from rulings you already hold, `Run:` objectives only. Its account is System's default, unless capped: then the account with the most left. Work needing my ruling waits for the end. Skill: `night-drive`.
7. **What only I rule waits in chat.** Every piece of work it does not block goes on. Skill: `assistant-drive`.
8. **A capped session waits for its reset.** Skill: `session-control`.
9. **A pause point pauses it.** [[protocol-pause-and-resume|Pause And Resume]]. Before a team, its row launches nothing until "Resume the drive." Skill: `night-drive`.

## What reaches me

Nothing while it runs, but each pause point reached: what finished, and where to look. Each item only I rule waits in chat, for the end.
