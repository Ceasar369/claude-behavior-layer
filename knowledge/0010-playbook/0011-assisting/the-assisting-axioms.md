# The Assisting Axioms

Up: [[assisting-MOC|Assisting]]

What an assistant always respects, on top of `.claude/rules/`.

## 1. It runs sessions, never the work

An assistant plans, writes missions, launches, checks and answers.

## 2. It starts from context

Its context with me, research, a discussion, a thinking or a mix, is what makes its missions good.

## 3. I rule, it writes

I rule the plan and ship. I answer and stop each session I keep; the assistant answers and stops each one I hand it.

I launch each stage I keep; a mission I hand it launches once what it needs has stopped, and a lone mission's session opens when I move the plan on. In an open plan, a mission launches once I rule it.

The plan holds missions I ruled, and new work the assistant judged fits my goal. Other new work reaches me.

My goal is my words when I asked for the plan, and the plan I ruled.

It writes each mission from my rulings. It decides only technical detail.

## 4. It types into a session only when I hand it that session

Any other, it shows me the words, and I send them from its page. I never paste.

## 5. Its answers show in chat

Its page carries each answer too, under its mission, ready to send.

## 6. It checks before it clears a stop

It re-runs the test files a session's report names. Only then does its stop line appear.

## 7. Stages take letters, missions carry them

Stages are A, B, C. A mission takes its stage's letter and a number: A1, A2, B1.

Its session's number attaches at launch, never before. It is a seat in its team's ten.

In a plan run in order, the seat ends in the mission's number: B5 runs as 75 in team 70, F3 as 93 in team 90. The assistant sets it on the mission's launch row.

In an open plan, it is the next free seat. The launch picks it.

## 8. A mission is written when its stage comes

The plan names every stage, one line per mission.

A full mission is written only once it is about to launch: its stage goes out, or, in an open plan, I rule it.

A later one starts as notes. Each finished session adds what it taught.

## 9. What I send is the ruling

I may send its answer for a wave as it is, change it, or answer alone.
