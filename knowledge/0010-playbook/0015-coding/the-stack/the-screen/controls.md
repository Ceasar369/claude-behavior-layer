# Controls

Up: [[the-screen-MOC|The Screen]]

The shape of a control a person presses.

A control holding words is a rectangle, its corners from the radius scale.
Never a pill, never a circle.

A press, a row or a card hovers to the surface. One with an edge also turns
its edge orange.

A link takes its sentence's size, and is underlined at rest. It is orange;
on hover, deep orange.

A standing setting, on or off, is a switch.
A tick a form sends, or a consent, is a checkbox.

A press never offers what the person's own record cannot give.

Switches group by subject. Each group stands under its own label.
