# Words

Up: [[the-screen-MOC|The Screen]]

The words every screen carries: its opening line, its presses, its empty places.

Every screen in the app shell opens with one line under its title. It says
what the screen holds, or what it asks. It shows at every width. Settings
and a record's page need no line under a title.

A button names its act, never "Submit".

A press that sends, binds or ends says what follows. One line says it, just
above the button. On a form, its send part's note is that line. A page
holding one press may say what follows in its opening line.

A press held back reads greyed out. Just above it, what holds it and why
stand together, once, in red, framed in red, with the press that frees it
at the right. A switch holding a whole page stands at its top.

An empty place says what will appear there. When the person can fill it,
its act follows the words.

A screen shows each fact once, at every width.

An act takes the word the product already has for it. A screen never coins
a new verb.
