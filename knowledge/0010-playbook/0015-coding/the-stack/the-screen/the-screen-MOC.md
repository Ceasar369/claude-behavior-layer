# the-screen — MOC

Up: [[the-stack-MOC|The Stack]]

What sits inside a screen, in every frontend: its width, its words, then each kind.

## Children

- [[width|Width]] — where a screen's content stands, and how wide.
- [[words|Words]] — a screen's opening line, its presses' names, its empty places.
- [[controls|Controls]] — the shape of a control a person presses.
- [[forms|Forms]] — a form's fields, notes, hints and marks.
- [[lists|Lists]] — a list's rows, columns, views, search and figures.
- [[a-records-page|A Record's Page]] — one record, read top to bottom.

> **Stand-in.** This project's own kinds, one document each.

- [[a-screens-own-list|A Screen's Own List]] — a screen keeping its pages apart, and listing them.

## Admission rule

A document enters here when it rules one kind of thing inside a screen, in every frontend.
