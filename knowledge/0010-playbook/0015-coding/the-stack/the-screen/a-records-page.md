# A Record's Page

Up: [[the-screen-MOC|The Screen]]

How a page showing one record reads, top to bottom.

A record's page shows one record.

It is one document, read top to bottom. Its sections stay on one page.

> **Stand-in.** What each of this project's records adds to its page.

The head spans the page's content, at every width.

Every part spans the page's content, as the head does. No side column
inside the page.

The part that holds a press wears its badge beside its name, and nothing
else.

A warning asking a person to check something before they act opens the
card it guards. It spans the card, in the warning colour, with a warning
sign, its words bold and larger than the card's.

A record's files show one row each. A row holds a small preview, or the
file's glyph, then its name over its size. The whole row opens the file. A
file the browser cannot show downloads instead, and its row says Download.
