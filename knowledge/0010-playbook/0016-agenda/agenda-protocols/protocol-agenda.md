# Protocol — Agenda

Up: [[agenda-protocols-MOC|Agenda Protocols]]

When the subject is my agenda: what goes on it, what waits on what, and what comes down.

## The parts it touches

Behaviour. `.claude/state/agenda/`: its `README.md`, its `topics.json`, and one file per item.

The README holds what the agenda is, what it is not, and an item's shape.

[[the-behaviour-axioms|The Behaviour Axioms]] hold the floor.

## The questions, in order

1. What changes? What I dictate is added. An item may fold into another, or split in two.
2. What does each item wait on? Name every link.

## The lessons

- Big work enters as items. A team of teams grows too big.
- An item names no session. Each named session is one more link to keep in step.
- Two items that must run side by side may deserve to be one.
- Where an item sits is the drawing's. Placed by hand, lines cross more.
