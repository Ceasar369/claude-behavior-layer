## The references

### Where it sits

> **Stand-in.** This project's references folder, by its full path.

It is not a git repository.

Its own front door is `90-references-MOC.md`, at its root.

That MOC lists every set. It is the authority on what is there.

### What it holds

Screen walks, competitor captures, source PDFs, copied source code.

Ours and other companies' alike. Every set names its date.

### What a capture is

A capture records what existed, on a date, never what was intended. It never becomes false.

### The vault holds none of it

A capture never enters `knowledge/`. Not the image, not the walk.

Research I wrote myself is a capture too.

A finding changes what we build. It leaves the capture behind.

### Reading one

"The references" names one folder and no other.

### Adding to it

One folder per subject, lowercase kebab-case.

The folder carries its own MOC, and the date it was walked.

Nothing is deleted from it.
