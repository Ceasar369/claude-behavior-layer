# The Vendor Policy

Up: [[securing-MOC|Securing]]

Which companies we trust with data, and how.

## Every company named

Each layer's vendor choice names every company it reaches, as [[the-vendor-glossary|The Vendor Glossary]] states.

## Read before trusted

We read a vendor's agreement before it holds our data.

It must say where the data sits, and who can reach it.

What a vendor's terms require of one side lives in that side's layer folder.
