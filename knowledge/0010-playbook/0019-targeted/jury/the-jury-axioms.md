# The Jury Axioms

Up: [[jury-MOC|Jury]]

What a session always respects when it puts words before a jury, on top of `.claude/rules/`.

A jury runs only on my word. Launch in the jury tab is my word.

A juror gets only the typed question: the context, one question, the answer's shape. Never my conversation, never my leaning.

Every answer carries a one-line reason, and a word-for-word quote with its place. Every shape allows "can't tell".

A quote the script cannot find never counts as its answer. A page it cannot fetch leaves the answer standing, marked.

A pick reads as a count, like 4 of 5 said no. Never a percentage.

A split stays split, each side's quote beside it on the jury tab. Never a majority win.

A juror with no answer is named in the count, never counted.

The conclusion adds no fact. Each sentence names the claim it rests on.

No other skill calls the jury.
