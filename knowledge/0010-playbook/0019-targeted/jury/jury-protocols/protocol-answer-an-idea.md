Up: [[jury-protocols-MOC|Jury Protocols]]

When the answer is an idea.

## The parts it touches

`.claude/tools/jury.py`: the jury sessions, and the lines a juror answers in.

[[the-jury-axioms|The Jury Axioms]] hold the floor.

## What a juror gets and returns

It gets the context and one question.

It returns one idea, its reason, and the quote it rests on, with its place.

## The questions, in order

1. What does the idea serve? The question names it.
2. How does it come back? Every idea listed whole, never counted.
