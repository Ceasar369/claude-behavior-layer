# jury-protocols — MOC

Up: [[jury-MOC|Jury]]

One protocol per answer shape: what a juror gets, and returns.

## Children

- [[protocol-answer-yes-or-no|Answer Yes Or No]] — When the answer is yes or no.
- [[protocol-answer-one-pick|Answer One Pick]] — When the answer is one pick, most often whether a claim holds against its source.
- [[protocol-answer-several-picks|Answer Several Picks]] — When the answer is every pick that applies, among named options.
- [[protocol-answer-an-idea|Answer An Idea]] — When the answer is an idea.
- [[protocol-answer-a-case|Answer A Case]] — When the answer is a case: should I do this, argued both ways.

## Admission rule

A protocol enters here when its subject is one answer shape, and I agree to it.

Every line opens with the kind of subject it serves.

A lesson I write joins its protocol as one line, under The lessons.
