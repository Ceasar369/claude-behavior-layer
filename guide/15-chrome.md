# 15 — Chrome

A session in your own Chrome, with your logins.

## What it needs

Google Chrome, and the Claude in Chrome extension in each profile you use. The extension signs into the account that drives your Chrome: account 1, unless your list names another, as 04 shows.

The route also needs `.claude/lib/launch.py`, iTerm2's Python API, and a second session. My walk met no prompt, in the pane or from the extension.

## Your first try

In any session:

```
/drive-chrome open https://example.com in a new tab, read its heading and its one link's address, then close the tab. Read only: click nothing, type nothing.
```

My line named no profile, so it took the Default one. In my earlier one-session walk, it opened that profile's window, worked in a tab of its own, then closed the tab. That route took two minutes and twenty seconds.

Now the session you ask researches the route and launches a clicking session on Chrome's account. That session runs on Opus at low effort. The first session stays with you, checks its work, and passes it each next step.

In that earlier walk, Chrome held one window before, and one after.

It found no heading: the page holds none now. Its one link, "Learn more", goes to `https://iana.org/help/example-domains`.

A site behind a login waits for your hands. Sign each one in, in its profile, before a session needs it.
