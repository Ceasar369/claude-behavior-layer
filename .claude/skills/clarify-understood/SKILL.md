# Clarify Understood

## Step 1 — Take the message

An argument → it is the message.

No argument → my last message before this one.

Read every file it names.

## Step 2 — Split it into instructions

One item per instruction, in my order.

One subject per turn: my message's first. The rest wait, in order.

Each says what I asked, with real values: files, numbers, names.

Say what I meant. Never my words back.

## Step 3 — Flag misheard words

A word that looks misheard becomes its own item, before the instruction it sits in.

"I read bus over as passover."

A word that fits nothing says so plainly.

## Step 4 — Lay them out in chat

One numbered item each, under a bold title.

Each carries what I meant, and nothing else. No For, no Against.

## Step 5 — Once everything is agreed

The agreed items are the confirmation.

My message asked for an act → do it. Nothing else goes first.

No act asked → say in one line that it landed, and stop.

## Never

Never act before every item is agreed.

Never add an opinion, a fix or a next step to what I said.

Never pass a misheard word through silently.
