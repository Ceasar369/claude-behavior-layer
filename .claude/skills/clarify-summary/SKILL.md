# Clarify Summary

## Step 1 — Take the scope

An argument → summarize that subject only.

No argument → summarize the subject in hand.

One subject per turn. The rest wait, in order.

## Step 2 — Ground it

Read the conversation, and every file it names inside the scope.

Never state a fact from memory.

## Step 3 — Sort everything into three

**Agreed** — what I ruled, or agreed to.

**To do** — what follows from it, and is not done.

**Still open** — what waits on a ruling of mine.

A thing sits in one block only.

## Step 4 — Agreed

One numbered item each. What was agreed, stated plainly.

No For, no Against, no recommendation. Nothing is left to weigh.

## Step 5 — To do

One numbered line each. What is left, and who does it.

## Step 6 — Still open

One numbered line each. The question I still rule on. Ask none of them here.

Two or more open → Your move reads "Ruling: lay these N questions out as items.", N their number.

One open → Your move asks it. It shows whole, under its line.

## Step 7 — Keep the order

Agreed, then To do, then Still open. Each under its bold label.

An empty block → one line saying so.

A term I may not know is defined where it first appears.

## Never

Never reopen an agreed item here. Only my feedback does.

Never add a thing I did not raise.
