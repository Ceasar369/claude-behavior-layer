# Clarify Full Picture

I remember nothing of this work. Not the session, its mission, or any turn.

Three sections tell one story. Each zooms into the one before.

## Step 1 — Take the scope

An argument → that work.

No argument → the work in hand.

## Step 2 — Ground it

Read the conversation, the mission, and every file the work names.

Never state a fact from memory.

## Step 3 — Find the story

Three parts, always in this order.

- **The work.** What it is, what it is for, and where it comes from: its mission, and who handed it.
- **Done.** What was built or done, and whether it is proven.
- **Needed.** What the work needs from me now.

Nothing needed → Needed says so.

## Step 4 — In short

Under the bold label **In short**. Three lines: the work, done, needed.

No insider word. A session's number and its mission's name stay.

```
Session 21 builds mission C1, from your assistant, session 20. It turns a customer's own spreadsheet into an invoice.
It is built and tested.
It needs a test server you rent for two hours.
```

## Step 5 — A bit more

Under the bold label **A bit more**. The same three parts, one short paragraph each, never more.

Each opens with its italic label: *The work.*, *Done.*, *Needed.*

It details In short. It adds why the work exists, and why it needs me.

A word I may not know is defined where it first appears.

## Step 6 — The full picture

Under the bold label **The full picture**.

It opens the small pieces inside A bit more, in their order.

A piece with nothing worth adding is skipped.

Each piece is quoted in italics, then detailed.

```
*"A rented computer with a graphics card."* One cloud server with a graphics card, billed by the hour. A new account may need support's approval for graphics cards.
```

## Step 7 — Close

Your move is what Needed asks of me.

## Never

Never open a subject the section before did not name.

Never make a section lean on the next. Read alone, each tells me my move.

Never list every step. That is `clarify-steps`.

Never open a new decision. That is `clarify-items`.
