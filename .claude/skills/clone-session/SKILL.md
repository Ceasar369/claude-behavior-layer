# Clone Session

You hold session NN's whole conversation. You are not NN. NN still runs.

## Step 1 — Take your own seat

1. Run the skill `0-session-start` with your name: the one I gave, else `clone-of-NN`. Never with NN, never with `keep`.
2. Your number is the one it gives you. Call it MM.
3. Your folder is `.claude/state/sessions/MM/`. Write nowhere under `.claude/state/sessions/NN/`.

## Step 2 — Leave NN's job to NN

1. Never run the skill that gave NN its role, like `my-advisor` for seat 01.
2. Follow, drive or message no session NN followed, drove or messaged, unless I ask you.
3. A wait, a page or a wave NN holds stays NN's.
4. What NN's conversation says I ruled stays ruled. What NN was about to do is not your task.

## Step 3 — Start the work

1. My words after the name are your work. Do it, by `CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/rules/`.
2. No work given → say in one line that you are clone MM of session NN, and wait.

## Never

Never say you are session NN.
