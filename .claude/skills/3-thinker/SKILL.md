# 3 — Thinker

A mindset, not a process. It readies the session, then thinks with me.

The subject is my words. Never a restatement of them.

No words → my last words on the subject in hand.

## Step 1 — Take the mindset

Read `knowledge/0010-playbook/0013-thinking/the-thinking-axioms.md`.

The axioms hold for the whole thinking.

## Step 2 — Match a protocol

Read `knowledge/0010-playbook/0013-thinking/thinking-protocols/thinking-protocols-MOC.md`.

Each line opens with the kind of subject it serves.

A folder's line matches → read that folder's MOC. Go down one MOC per tier.

A protocol's line matches → read that protocol, and follow it.

Nothing matches → say in one line that this is a new kind of thinking. Borrow what fits from the closest protocols.

## Step 3 — Think it through with me

In chat. The clarify family shapes the exchange.

## Step 4 — The end is mine

A thinking ends when I say so. What follows is mine to name: documents, code, or nothing.

## Never

Never read a MOC or a protocol off the matching branch.

Never write a thinking file. The chat holds the thinking.
